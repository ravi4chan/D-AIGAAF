# 01-Strategy and Governance Templates

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the template set for **D-AIGAAF Module 01 — Strategy & Governance**.

These templates translate strategic intent, governance policy and organisational accountability into structured records that can be used to establish, operate and review defence AI governance.

The templates support:

- AI strategy;
- governance structures;
- decision rights;
- risk governance;
- policy alignment;
- governance committees;
- escalation;
- accountability;
- strategic oversight;
- governance performance.

They are intended to complement, not replace, the governance requirements defined in Module 01.

# 12. Template 01-010 — AI Governance Review Record

## Purpose

Provides a structured periodic review of governance effectiveness.

## Review Scope

Assess:

- governance structure;
- decision rights;
- risk governance;
- policy alignment;
- committee effectiveness;
- assurance;
- incidents;
- changes;
- workforce capability;
- outstanding actions.

## Review Questions

1. Are governance responsibilities clear?
2. Are decision rights appropriate?
3. Are consequential risks reaching the correct authority?
4. Are controls operating effectively?
5. Are assurance findings being acted upon?
6. Are governance decisions traceable?
7. Are external requirements changing?
8. Has the operating environment changed?
9. Has AI capability or autonomy changed?
10. Does the governance model remain fit for purpose?

## Findings

Record:

- strengths;
- weaknesses;
- nonconformities;
- risks;
- improvement opportunities;
- corrective actions.

# 14. Governance Evidence

Governance records should provide evidence that:

- authority was defined;
- decisions were made by authorised persons;
- relevant information was considered;
- risks were identified;
- assurance was considered;
- conditions were established;
- dissent was captured where material;
- decisions were recorded;
- follow-up occurred.

A governance record should not merely demonstrate that a meeting took place.

It should demonstrate that governance actually occurred.

# 17. Governance Anti-Patterns

The templates should help detect and prevent:

### Governance by committee presence

A committee existing does not demonstrate effective governance.

### Responsibility without authority

A role should not be accountable for decisions it cannot control.

### Authority without evidence

Decision makers should receive sufficient evidence for consequential decisions.

### Technical ownership becoming operational authority

Technical expertise does not automatically confer operational authority.

### Risk acceptance by default

Unresolved risk must not silently become accepted risk.

### Compliance-only governance

Meeting documentation requirements is not equivalent to governing the AI capability.

### Ambiguous accountability

Consequential decisions must have identifiable human authority.

# 18. Minimum Governance Package

For a significant or consequential AI capability, the minimum governance package should normally include:

- approved governance model;
- role/responsibility matrix;
- decision rights;
- risk governance record;
- applicable policy/regulatory alignment;
- governance decisions;
- relevant committee record;
- escalation records where applicable;
- periodic governance review.

Additional records should be required according to mission consequence, autonomy and applicable requirements.

---

# 19. Final Principle

Strategy & Governance templates establish the **human and organisational architecture** within which D-AIGAAF operates.

The governing principle is:

> **AI governance is not the delegation of responsibility to an AI system. It is the deliberate allocation of authority, accountability, risk ownership and oversight to humans and institutions that remain responsible for the consequences of AI-enabled decisions and actions.**

The templates should therefore make the following visible:

**Who decides → On what evidence → Under what authority → Accepting what risk → Subject to what conditions → With what accountability → Reviewed how and when.**
